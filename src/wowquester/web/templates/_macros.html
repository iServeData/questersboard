What naming your character means

On: your character's name appears on this event's public roster, on the event card in Discord, and in any Chronicle story written about the event.

Off: you're shown as "a tank", "a healer" and so on, or counted as "+1 other" on the Discord card. Hosts and officers still see your character so they can organise the group.

Your Discord name and ID are never shown publicly. You can change this per event, set your default under My events → Privacy, and stop being named in a published story at any time.
