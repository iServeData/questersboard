Privacy

{% if lg.effective_date %}

Last updated {{ lg.effective_date }}

{% endif %}

QuestersBoard is the event planner and Chronicle for {{ cfg["community"]["name"] }}'s World of Warcraft players. It is run by {{ operator }} on the community's own server. This page explains, in plain terms, what it keeps about you and why.

The short version

  • We keep only what the planner needs: who you are on Discord, the characters you add, and the events you join.
  • No ads, no tracking scripts, no selling or sharing your data.
  • Nothing about you appears in a public Chronicle story unless you have said yes.

What we keep

  • Your Discord identity. When you log in with Discord we ask only for the identify permission. That gives us your Discord user ID, username and display name (the username lets hosts add you as a co-host by name). We do not see your email, your servers or your messages{% if cfg["item_links"]["enabled"] %} through login (item cards are explained below){% endif %}. Your community roles are checked through the community bot when you use QuestersBoard; they aren't stored as a profile.
  • Your characters. What you type in: name, game edition, region, realm or ruleset, faction, race, class, level, spec, preferred role and any notes. You can remove a character at any time.
  • Events. Events you plan, co-host, sign up for or withdraw from, and attendance that an organiser records.
  • Chronicle stories. Story drafts, their revisions and review decisions.{% if cfg["solo_stories"]["enabled"] %} For a solo adventure, also the character, day and kind of adventure you chose, kept as a private record that is never listed or posted.{% endif %}
    • {% if cfg["solo_stories"]["enabled"] and cfg["solo_stories"]["join_me"] %}
  • Join me next time. If you press it on someone's solo story, we keep that you asked. The author gets a DM with your Discord name and sees you on their list, and you get a DM if they plan an event from the story. Nobody else sees it, and you can take it back on the same page.
    • {% endif %}
  • Screenshots. Images you upload to a story (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to {{ "%g" % (up.max_bytes / 1000000) }} MB and {{ up.max_per_entry }} per story). We re-encode every image, which removes hidden metadata such as camera details and location.
  • A login cookie. One cookie keeps you logged in for up to {{ cfg["web"]["session_hours"] }} hours. It is only used for this site, it isn't readable by page scripts, and we store only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it.
  • Short-lived technical records. To stop abuse we count requests per minute, which can briefly involve your IP address. A security log records actions such as creating, approving or removing things, and who did them.
  • Calendar links. If you create a personal calendar link, we store only a hashed copy, and you can revoke it.
    • {% if cfg["voice"]["enabled"] %}
  • Event voice channels. While an event is running, its voice channel lets in the people signed up, and we note on the channel who has access. We don't record who talks, what is said or how long anyone stays; the bot only counts how many people are in the channel.{% if cfg["voice"]["guest_invites"] %} If someone opens a guest voice link, Discord asks them to let us see their Discord name and add them to the {{ cfg["community"]["name"] }} server. We keep their Discord ID only while the event runs and delete it when it ends; the sign-in permission is used once and not kept, and no QuestersBoard profile is made for them.{% endif %} If a host removes or bans someone from the channel, the security log records it.
    • {% endif %} {% if cfg["item_links"]["enabled"] %}
  • Item cards. In the Discord channels set up for item cards, the community bot reads new messages only to find item names written in [square brackets], then posts a card with that item's game details. We don't keep the messages or who wrote them; only the item names and a link to the message are kept, for up to {{ ret.outbox_done_days }} days, and looked-up item details are kept as cached game data. If you delete your message, its card is removed too.
    • {% endif %}

What the public can see

Public pages (the event list, the public calendar and the Chronicle) show event details and head counts. They never show Discord names or IDs.

When you join an event you choose whether to name your character. If you do, your character's name appears on that event's roster, on its event card in Discord, and in any Chronicle story about it. If you don't, you're shown as "a tank", "a healer" and so on, or counted as "+1 other". Hosts and officers still see your character so they can organise the group. Naming is off unless you turn it on. You can change it per event, set your default under My events → Privacy, and press Stop naming me in this story on a published story at any time; that takes effect straight away.

Who else is involved

  • Discord handles login and the community bot, under Discord's privacy policy.
    • {% if cfg["blizzard"]["enabled"] %}
  • Blizzard. QuestersBoard looks up public game data (such as realms and classes) from Blizzard's API.{% if cfg["blizzard"]["battlenet_link_enabled"] %} If you choose to sync with Battle.net, you sign in on Blizzard's own page (we never see your Battle.net password), we read your character list once (names, level, race, class, faction and ruleset) and don't keep your Battle.net login. The list is stored encrypted only while you pick characters to add ({{ cfg["blizzard"].get("battlenet_import_minutes", 60) }} minutes), then deleted. Forget my Battle.net link removes the verified mark from your characters at any time.{% endif %} If you import details for a character (gear, talents, professions and the recipes it knows, stats, portrait, PvP, progress, hunter pets, guild), you tick which groups to bring in and choose who can see each one: only you, members of your in-game guild who also use QuestersBoard, or everyone signed in to QuestersBoard. Sharing beyond yourself only applies while the character is verified as yours through Battle.net; otherwise it stays private. Characters whose owners haven't imported and shared details never appear on guild or member pages. Pictures (portraits, hunter pet pictures, and gear and talent icons) are fetched from Blizzard's image server by QuestersBoard and shown from this site, so your browser doesn't contact Blizzard; a picture's plain background may be removed or trimmed for display, and nothing else about it is changed. Details are refreshed when you press Refresh from Blizzard (one character) or Refresh all from Blizzard.{% set every = cfg["blizzard"].get("auto_refresh_hours", 4) %}{% if every %} QuestersBoard also refreshes them {{ "every hour" if every == 1 else "once a day" if every == 24 else "every " ~ every ~ " hours" }}, along with the Blizzard check on characters that have one, using the groups and sharing choices you already made; it never brings in a group you left out, never shares anything more widely, and never looks up a character you entered yourself without a Blizzard check. If Blizzard no longer has a character, what came from Blizzard is deleted and the character stays as you entered it.{% endif %} After a QuestersBoard update the organisers may also refresh everyone's imported details so they keep displaying correctly; that uses the groups and sharing choices you already made, never brings in a group you left out, never shares anything more widely, and leaves characters without imported details alone. Remove imported details deletes them at once; removing the character or using Remove me does too. Blizzard data is kept no longer than {{ cfg["blizzard"].get("data_ttl_days", 30) }} days after its last refresh (yours, the organisers'{% if cfg["blizzard"].get("auto_refresh_hours", 4) %} or the automatic one{% endif %}): a Blizzard check that isn't refreshed by then is cleared, and the character stays as you entered it. Blizzard data is provided "as is", without any guarantee that it's complete, accurate or available. Blizzard's own handling of your data is covered by the Blizzard Privacy Policy. QuestersBoard isn't made, endorsed or supported by Blizzard.
    • {% endif %} {% if cfg["ai"]["enabled"] %}
  • Writing helper. If you press the suggest-wording button, the draft text you're writing is sent to an AI service to suggest wording. Nothing is published automatically; you decide what to keep.
    • {% endif %}

Nobody else receives your data. People with administrator access to the community server can technically read the database, as with any self-hosted service.

How long we keep things

  • Login sessions: until they expire, plus {{ ret.session_days_after_expiry }} day{{ "s" if ret.session_days_after_expiry != 1 }}.
  • Withdrawn or rejected signups for past events: {{ ret.withdrawn_signup_days }} days.
    • {% if cfg["voice"]["enabled"] %}
  • Voice access, guest voice links, guests and voice bans for an event: until the event ends.
    • {% endif %}
  • Cached game data: {{ ret.api_cache_days }} days. Finished background jobs: {{ ret.outbox_done_days }} days.
  • Security log: {{ ret.audit_days }} days.
  • Encrypted backups: {{ lg.backup_keep_days }} days.
  • Published Chronicle stories stay up until they are unpublished.
    • {% if cfg["solo_stories"]["enabled"] and cfg["solo_stories"]["join_me"] %}
  • Join me next time requests: until you take them back, the story is deleted, or you use Remove me.
    • {% endif %}
  • Your Discord identity, characters and event history: for as long as you use QuestersBoard, or until you use Remove me.

Your choices

  • Remove your characters and withdraw from events at any time.
  • Turn Chronicle naming off for any event, or stop being named in a published story.
  • Remove me. Under My settings → Remove me you can delete your QuestersBoard profile yourself. It shows exactly what will happen and asks you to type "remove me" first. It withdraws you from every event, cancels upcoming events you host (telling the people signed up), deletes your characters, replaces your names in Chronicle stories with a neutral description such as "a healer", and disconnects your Discord login. It takes effect straight away and can't be undone. The security log ages out after {{ ret.audit_days }} days and backups after {{ lg.backup_keep_days }} days.
  • If you can no longer log in (for example, you've left the Discord), ask an organiser and they'll do the same removal for you.
  • Ask for a copy of what we hold about you.
